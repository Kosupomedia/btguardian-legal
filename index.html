BTGUARDIAN™

Bluetooth Connection Guard

PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: October 2, 2026

Developer: Kosupo Media
Owner: Troy Bond

1. Overview

BTGUARDIAN is an Android application designed to monitor supported Bluetooth connection events, display device status, and provide connection notifications.

2. Information Accessed

To operate, BTGUARDIAN may access:

  • Names and Bluetooth addresses of paired or connected devices.
  • Connection and disconnection events.
  • User-selected protected devices.
  • Temporary allowance settings.
  • Application preferences and monitoring status.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to identify selected devices, display connection status, send notifications, and restore saved settings.

4. Local Storage

BTGUARDIAN stores selected device information and application preferences locally on the Android device.

The intended release does not require a user account or transmit Bluetooth device information to a developer-operated server.

5. Information Sharing

BTGUARDIAN is not designed to sell personal information or share Bluetooth device information with advertisers.

Android and device manufacturers may process information as part of their operating-system services.

6. Permissions

Bluetooth and Nearby Devices permissions support device identification and connection monitoring. Notification permissions and foreground-service capabilities support alerts and background monitoring.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Saved selections and preferences remain until modified, removed, or cleared. Users may remove devices within BTGUARDIAN or clear application storage through Android settings.

Uninstalling ordinarily removes private local application data, subject to Android backup and restore behavior.

8. Data Security

BTGUARDIAN relies on Android application storage and operating-system permissions. Absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

9. Children's Privacy

BTGUARDIAN is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically designed for children.

10. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when functionality or data practices change. Material revisions will be reflected in an updated policy.
